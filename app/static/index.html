Connect your iCloud session.

  1. Install Cookie-Editor for Chrome.
  2. Sign in at icloud.com. Choose Trust this browser / device when prompted.
  3. Stay on the signed-in iCloud tab and open Cookie-Editor. If permission is requested, select This site.
  4. Click Export → Header String. The cookies are copied to your clipboard.
  5. Paste the result below. Do not choose JSON. Treat these cookies as account credentials; never share them.

Keep the server running 24/7 with Internet access so it can attempt session refreshes every 90-180 seconds and maintain the reserve. Sleep, shutdown or outages interrupt this. Apple can still expire or revoke the session; export fresh cookies if needed.

You can explore without connecting iCloud and add your cookies later in Settings.
